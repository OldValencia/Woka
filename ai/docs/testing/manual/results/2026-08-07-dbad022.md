## What the fixes changed

- **TC-PIP-02 (a)** — `MediaViewerContent` takes `panRequiresCtrl`, set only by
  `MediaViewerFullscreenSurface`. With it on, the plain drag is not installed at all and
  `mediaZoomAndButtonPan` accepts `Ctrl + LMB` alongside `Ctrl + RMB`. The main viewer is unchanged.
- **TC-PIP-02 (b)** — `pipWindowDraggable` moved to `PointerEventPass.Initial` and consumes once the
  cursor has travelled more than `DRAG_SLOP_PX` **screen** pixels, which cancels the tap detector
  below it. The slop is deliberately not `viewConfiguration.touchSlop`: that is measured in the
  composable's own coordinates, and this modifier drags the window along under the cursor, so the
  local travel stays near zero however far the window goes. A first attempt that used the local
  distance was written, tested and thrown away for exactly that reason.
- **TC-SYS-01** — `app/navigation/StartupRoute.kt` resolves the launch argument, `Router.start`
  applies it, `initKoin(launchArgs)` calls both before the first composition, and
  `Screen.Folder.offerPin` drives `FolderPinPrompt` in the dock's top slot.
- **TC-SYS-06** — `core/utils/copyFilesToClipboard` (expect/actual), used by the grid's Copy and by
  the viewer; `MediaViewerUtils.kt` is gone.
