The grid tile for an audio file

Centre

  • Cover: square, up to 300 dp (46 % of the height), radius 12, 1 dp ring, a soft drop shadow.
  • A click on the cover plays / pauses.
  • Paused: the cover settles to 94 % and dims slightly (brightness 0.88), 350 ms.
  • Title 20 sp semibold; the artist 13 sp text2. Album, year, genre, codec, sample rate, bitrate and length live in the Info panel, not here.
  • No tags: title = file name without extension; second line = the folder's name.
  • Behind it, the cover blurred and dimmed — the viewer's ambient backdrop, 35 % (22 % light). No cover → no backdrop.

Controls bar

  • The video bar exactly — 48 dp, radius 16, surfaceElevated at 0.5 — minus subtitles and fullscreen.
  • Play/pause, elapsed, seek slider (no picture preview on hover), remaining, volume, ⚙.
  • ⚙ keeps Repeat, Shuffle, Speed and the time label; video tracks, deinterlace, ambient fill, chapters and sync are not shown.

Moving between files

  • The side arrows, the keyboard arrows and auto-advance step through the folder's audio files only.
  • The carousel shows those audio files as cover tiles.
  • Closing the viewer does not stop the music — the track goes on in Now Playing.

Grid tile

  • Cover, cropped square; a note glyph and the length bottom-left, as a video tile carries its duration.
  • No cover: the placeholder, with the file name on the tile so a folder of bare files can still be told apart.
  • No hover reel.

Settled with the author, 2026-11-01

  • Side arrows only — no previous / next buttons in the bar.
  • No format line under the title; it belongs to the Info panel.
  • Paused cover shrinks and dims a touch; a click on the cover toggles play.
