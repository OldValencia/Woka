Spec

  • One weight for the whole app; a glyph that stands for an on state (play, pause, the speaker levels, the filled bells, the clear button) takes fill.
  • File names stay as they are, so Icons and EditorIcons keep their constants. Crop, which shares Fit's file today, gets crop.svg of its own.
  • Cropped drops 16 of Phosphor's 256 units each side, so a glyph fills its box the way a tightly cropped SF Symbol does and no call site changes its dp size.
  • fill="currentColor" becomes fill="black" on import; the app tints every icon with a colour filter either way.
  • Unused, deleted: backward, backward_fill, bell_badge_slash, bell_badge_slash_fill, bolt_fill, ellipsis, forward, forward_fill, line_3_horizontal, magnifyingglass_exclamation_mark, repeat, repeat_one, speaker, speaker_badge_exclamationmark_fill, speaker_slash, speaker_wave_1, speaker_wave_2, speaker_wave_3, square_stack_3d_up, textformat.
  • Kept: woka_logo (ours) and music_note (drawn for AP-003).
