A folder card should read as a folder

Since the borrowed cover landed, a folder and a photograph both draw a photograph. Candidate 1 of the ticket says the answer is already on the card and merely under-drawn: the stack edge. home-proposal.html:131-133 specified two sheets darker than the tile, each with a hairline; FolderCardBody.kt:87-88 ships them filled with surfaceElevated — the tile's own colour — and no outline. This file settles the paint, in both themes, before any Kotlin moves. The geometry is not reopened: 8 / 15 px inset, 4 / 8 px of protrusion, 11 px bottom radius, drawn behind the tile.

Settled by hand: variant C. The two screens further down carry three optional additions on top of it — an inset cover, a fan on hover, a warm outline — because telling a folder from a photograph and making Home pleasant are two different questions, and only the second one is answered by looking at a screen where every tile is a folder.

The numbers

C is what ships. Two new Colors fields per theme for the fills, and an outline that Compose cannot draw with border() — that modifier strokes all four sides, and the top one is not optional here: on a sheet 4 dp tall, a hairline top and bottom leaves 2 dp of fill and the sheet becomes a contour. So the outline is a three-sided Path with the bottom corners rounded, stroked at 1 dp. Roughly twenty lines inside StackEdge; FolderCardBody.kt goes from 102 lines to about 127, inside the mandate.

Variant C — what shipsDarkLightSits between
stackEdge — near sheet, inset 8 #1A1E27 #E9E9EF card and canvas, on the card's side
stackEdgeFar — far sheet, inset 15 #141821 #DEDEE6 one step further out, still short of the canvas
outline, near / far rgba(255,255,255,.07) / .05 rgba(0,0,0,.09) / .06 left, bottom and right only — never the top
geometry (unchanged) inset 8dp / 15dp · 4dp visible each · bottom radius 9dp · behind the tile as already shipped

And what each switch above costs on top of C:

AdditionNumbersCost in Kotlin
Inset cover 6 dp mount, inner radius 5 dp, scrim and caption inset with it FolderCardPreview.kt + FolderCardCaption.kt: a padding and a second shape. No new colour — the mount is the tile's own surfaceElevated.
Fan on hover inset 8→6 and 15→12, protrusion 4→6 and 8→12, 200 ms, the card's own easing two animateDpAsState on the isHovered FolderCardBody already receives. Nothing at rest changes.
Warm hairline outline becomes accentFolders @ 34 / 20 % dark, 50 / 32 % light free — it replaces the outline colour C already computes. Drops the two neutral outline alphas instead of adding anything.
